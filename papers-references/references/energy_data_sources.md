# Candidate open data sources

This is a discovery checklist, not an endorsement or guarantee of completeness. Verify current access, terms, units, coverage and update cadence before analysis.

| Source | Typical data | Checks before use |
|---|---|---|
| Eurostat | Comparable European energy, household and price statistics | Table code, country definitions, revisions and units |
| International Energy Agency | Energy balances and indicators | Licensing, publication access and methodology |
| U.S. Energy Information Administration | U.S. energy prices, supply and demand | Geography, frequency and series notes |
| World Bank Data | Country-level development and energy indicators | Metadata, missingness and aggregation level |
| Copernicus Climate Data Store | Historical/reanalysis climate data | Product version, spatial resolution and time zone |
| National statistical or regulatory portals | National prices, consumption and policy series | Data dictionary, revisions and reuse conditions |

Prefer primary sources. Store raw downloaded material outside the repository unless its license and privacy status are clear. A URL alone does not prove that a particular number was present in the source.
