# Intent interview prompts

Ask one question at a time and capture the user's decision and remaining uncertainty.

1. What question should the analysis answer, and who will use the answer?
2. What period, geography and population are in scope?
3. Which source-backed fact makes the question timely or useful?
4. What comparison or outcome would make the analysis informative?
5. Which interpretations, claims or populations must be excluded?
6. What data or assumption is still missing, and who can approve it?
